# A9 – Pulleys

## Introduction

The assignment for this week is to design a pulley system using different concepts learned in lecture. 

This assignment has a list of parameters, both pulleys must have a 3” diameter, spin between 1000-2000 rpm, a belt tension of 4-6 lbf (static), and the pulleys can be made of steel, ABS or aluminum with a safety factor of 3. We are also assuming the belt is made from polyurethane rubber; which has a yield strength of 3.25 MPA at 20% strain. 

For my project, my operational speed will be 1200 rpm with 4 lbf of tension. Both pulleys will be made from aluminum.
