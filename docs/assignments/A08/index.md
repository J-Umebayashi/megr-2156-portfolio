## Reflections

Once again timing really bites me with this assignment. I had three midterms the week of this assignment which caused me to push this assignment back and the lack of timing has definitely affected this assignment. This assignment helped me get more comfortable with pulling equations from the Machinery’s Handbook and actually using them. My CAD modeling ended up better than I expected. There are parts of it I’m really not proud of and if I had more time I would remodel with better clearances. I think my lack of a detailed sketch also lead to me having to fix parts of my CAD model on the fly, and ended up costing me a ton of time. In hindsight I should’ve been less lazy and actually drew a thought out design before going straight into Solidworks.
