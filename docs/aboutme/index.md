What does it mean to defend an engineering decision; and do you currently know how to do it?

To defend an engineering decision is to have the ability to showcase your design choices, receive constructive criticism from your peers, and be able to have a dialogue explaining why you made the choices you made. Defending decisions is the toughest part, as you must be ready to show all the calculations, simulations, and different tests you ran on your design. If you cannot show any data or reasons to why you made your decisions, you cannot truly defend your design. Even if your tests are incorrect or were applied in the wrong context, showing you thought of those considerations whilst designing is way easier to defend then showing you put no thought into a design. I think I'm currently know how to defend a decision, but I don't think I am that good at it. I need more practice in presenting my decisions and explaining my data and processes with more confidence.
