#About Me
Jake Umebayashi

Hello, my name is Jake Umebayashi, I am studying Mechanical Engineering with a Motorsport Concentration at UNC Charlotte. I am pursuing this degree to have a career in the Motorsports industry after I graduate from school. As an engineer, I am very thorough in my design processes and decisions. I strive for well-planned and executed solutions to problems I encounter. I am not afraid to make mistakes or hear criticism about my designs or my choices. I believe that I learn better from taking criticism and feedback from other engineers, and making mistakes is sometimes the only way to truly learn what to do and not to do. Mistakes can also reveal important considerations that weren’t previously obvious. I have always been fascinated by mechanical systems, the design behind them, and learning about how systems work and interact with other systems. Once I discovered and fell in love with motorsport, I found an avenue where I could solve mechanical engineering problems while competing against others, a combination of many things I am passionate about. I am becoming the kind of engineer that is aware of the real world and not just stuck in a CAD file on a computer. I believe my abilities to manufacture and assemble parts has an influence on my design influences and choices; while also keeping me grounded in the constraints I must work around while designing.
